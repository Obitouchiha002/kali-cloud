    • Questions

    Is this really a full operating system?

    Yes. It's the genuine Kali Linux OS running in an isolated container on our servers, with the whole XFCE desktop streamed to your browser. It is not a simulation or a limited terminal.

    Do I need to install anything?

    No. It runs entirely in your browser tab. Even a low-end laptop works — the stream quality adapts to your device automatically.

    Is it legal? Can I get in trouble?

    Every box is sealed off from the real internet, so you can only practice against the targets we provide. You must agree to our Acceptable Use policy — the platform is for learning and authorized testing only.

    Why can't I do WiFi/Bluetooth attacks on the free plan?

    Those need physical radio hardware, which cloud servers don't have. Real wireless labs run on dedicated hardware and are offered on the Hardware tier.

    Is my activity private?

    Your box is isolated and destroyed after your session. On Pro, storage is per-user and encrypted. See our Privacy Policy for details.
